Neon Shield is a fast-paced arcade survival game built around one idea: bounce back. You pilot a lone spaceship with no weapons, only an energy shield that you rotate around yourself with the mouse. Enemy fighters surround you and open fire from every direction, and your job is to time your shield perfectly and send their bullets straight back. Every deflected shot doubles in speed and becomes a devastating laser that destroys the enemies who fired it. Each hit you take chips away a piece of your shield, and when it finally breaks, you get one last chance: answer a general-knowledge question about space, Earth or computer science within 10 seconds. Answer correctly and you bounce back into the fight with a fresh shield, a moment of invincibility and the enemies pushed away. Answer wrong, or run out of time, and it’s game over. The longer you survive, the faster and more relentless the enemies become, so every run is a race to beat your high score.
